PMO Bot

NWC Northwestern Cluster · weekly project control

Ask for the latest weekly update of any project — by PO, name, contractor, status or area. The bot opens as a pop-up in the bottom-right corner of your screen, and you can pin it to the Windows taskbar with its avatar icon.

Pin the PMO Bot to the Windows taskbar

  1. Microsoft Edge: click the ⋯ menu (top-right) → Apps → Install this site as an app. Chrome: ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  2. In the dialog tick Pin to taskbar and click Install.
  3. From then on, click the PMO Bot avatar on your taskbar. It opens in its own small window — right-click the icon for quick links to the Portfolio, Troubled and Delayed projects.
